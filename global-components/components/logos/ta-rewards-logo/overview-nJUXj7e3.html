Guidelines

The Rewards Program Logo should create a clear visual distinction that helps members recognize rewards-related content while maintaining connection to the broader Tripadvisor brand family.

 

 

 

Anatomy & Size

 

Anatomy

The Tripadvisor Rewards logo is a structured lockup consisting of our symbol (“Ollie”) and the wordmark “Rewards.”

 

Size

Based on platform use, the rewards program logo comes in three different sizes.

 

 

 

Appearance

When placed on surface colors, the “Ollie” symbol and colors will invert.
